# Avelith site artwork

These two images were generated with the built-in image generation tool as original visual studies for Avelith. Source PNGs are in this directory; optimized, responsive WebP copies are in `public/assets/site/`.

## Hero artwork

Mode: generate a new raster image.

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: compact featured art tile in the hero of Avelith, an independent multidisciplinary creative studio. Primary request: a captivating abstract editorial still that expresses connected creative disciplines. Subject: one sculptural continuous ribbon of brushed champagne metal and deep teal enamel, looping through a warm ivory architectural space and casting precise geometric shadows. Style: premium art direction, photorealistic CGI with restrained luxury, tactile materials and elegant imperfections, gallery quality. Composition: landscape 4:3 crop safe with central sculptural subject and rich detail even at small card size. Lighting: raking afternoon sun, high contrast, subtle gold reflections. Color palette: cream stone, charcoal shadow, petrol teal, brushed gold. Constraints: no letters, text, logos, watermark, interface, people, or browser frame.

## Studio artwork

Mode: generate a new raster image.

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: large editorial image in a multidisciplinary creative studio manifesto section. Primary request: show the tangible practice of a high-end design studio in one extraordinary still life. Scene: long dark walnut worktable beside a sunlit plaster wall, carefully composed with sculptural packaging mockups, large typographic print samples with abstract non-letter forms, architectural maquette, warm stone and metal material swatches, folded fabric, color studies and a tablet displaying a simple teal-gold abstract graphic. No people. Style: world-class interior and design editorial photography, believable objects, sophisticated restraint and depth, not generic office stock. Composition: wide landscape with focal arrangement across the frame, layered foreground and background, room for crop. Lighting: warm directional window light, natural shadows, rich texture. Palette: charcoal walnut, ivory, muted teal and sparing brass. Constraints: no readable text, no logos, no watermark, no browser frame.
