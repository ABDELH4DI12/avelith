# Avelith Studio V6 — Visual sources & usage notes

This prototype deliberately separates **Avelith capability studies** from claimed client work.

## Avelith-owned / project-local assets
- Optimized WebP copies of the credited Pexels photos are stored in `public/assets/gallery/`; compressed video copies and poster frames are in `public/assets/motion/`.
- `public/assets/avelith-mark-transparent.png` — extracted from the Avelith brand identity board created for this project; used in the navbar and preloader.
- `public/assets/graphic-shapes-reference.png` — visual direction supplied by the user in this conversation; used as a local Graphic Design reference.
- All six Website cards combine original HTML/CSS Avelith concept layouts with AI-generated editorial imagery created for this site. They are capability studies, not screenshots of client projects.
- The full-size generated source images are in `design-assets/web-concepts/`; responsive WebP versions served by the site are in `public/assets/web-concepts/`.
- The hero and studio editorial images are original AI-generated visual studies for this site. Their source PNGs and prompts are in `design-assets/site/`; responsive WebP copies are in `public/assets/site/`.
